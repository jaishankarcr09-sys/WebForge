# V4 first-batch proposed pair review

These are assistant-proposed labels based on the official product pages linked in the CSV. They are **not user-approved or expert-verified ground truth** and must not be merged into the training dataset automatically.

## Rule applied
Label 1 only where a buyer could plausibly choose either product to solve the same primary problem. Rationale and official-page URLs are recorded per pair. Re-check product scope before accepting.

## Batch
Four high-confidence candidate alternatives were selected from the 66-pair worksheet:
- Basecamp vs Wrike — project/work management
- HubSpot vs Salesforce — CRM
- Zendesk vs Intercom — customer service/helpdesk
- Vercel vs Netlify — frontend/web application deployment

The proposal CSV deliberately uses `assistant_proposed` status and is separate from the blank reviewer worksheet. Keep the reviewer label blank until the reviewer explicitly accepts, edits, or rejects each proposal.
